Participant Information Sheet & Consent Form

Study on Human Evaluation of AI-Based Candidate Ranking Systems

1. Purpose of the Study

This study investigates how people evaluate and interact with AI-based candidate ranking systems in a recruitment context. Participants will be asked to review candidate profiles and AI-generated rankings, and to provide their assessments and feedback.

2. Who Can Participate

Participation is open to adults (18+) who are willing to engage with the study tasks online. No prior experience with AI or recruitment is required.

3. What Participation Involves

You will be asked to complete an online questionnaire and review a set of candidate profiles ranked by an AI system. The session is expected to take approximately 20–30 minutes.

4. Voluntary Participation & Right to Withdraw

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without giving a reason and without any consequences. Data collected up to the point of withdrawal may still be used in anonymised form.

5. Data Collection & Privacy

All data collected will be anonymised and stored securely. No personally identifiable information will be published. Data will be used solely for academic research purposes and will be retained for a maximum of five years in line with institutional data management policies.

6. Risks & Benefits

There are no known risks associated with participation beyond those of everyday internet use. Your participation contributes to research on fair and transparent AI systems in hiring.
